GLASGOW 2026

India finishes 4th on the medal table

India completed its Glasgow 2026 campaign with 39 medals, including 13 gold, 17 silver and 9 bronze medals.

The Indian contingent competed across the ten-sport programme and produced memorable performances in weightlifting, boxing, athletics, judo and para sports.

13 Gold
17 Silver
9 Bronze

Key Highlights

Mirabai's Golden Moment

Mirabai Chanu won India's first gold medal in Glasgow and secured her third consecutive Commonwealth Games title.

Historic Athletics

Gulveer Singh became the first Indian track and field athlete to win two individual medals at one Games.

New Judo Milestone

Asmita Dey became India's first Commonwealth Games gold medallist in judo.

From record-breaking performances to historic firsts, India's Glasgow 2026 campaign showcased determination, talent and sporting excellence.
